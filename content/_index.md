# About me

Hi, I'm Beth, I'm a product manager and technical writer. I like working in complex technical domains, in particular on figuring out how to make infrastructure useful and useable. I'm happiest when I'm learning something new.

I grew up in London, and now live in Zürich, Switzerland. I work at Google; previously I worked at [Redgate Software](http://www.red-gate.com/), [Ocado Technology](http://www.ocadotechnology.com/), [Improbable](https://improbable.io), [Digital Asset](http://digitalasset.com). [More about my work experience]({{< relref "cv" >}}).

I'm one of the co-founders of [Coffeehouse Conference](https://www.coffeehouseconf.com/).

I was previously the editor of the monthly [Write the Docs newsletter](http://www.writethedocs.org/newsletter/). Sometimes I give [talks at conferences and meetups]({{< relref "talks" >}}).
